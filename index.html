What is Viva Engage?

Viva Engage is an internal employee experience platform within Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Teams designed to connect Associates.

02 / HOW TO ACCESS VIVA ENGAGE EST. 2 MINS

How to Access Viva Engage

Viva Engage is accessible through your browser via your Microsoft 365 profile or directly within Microsoft Teams.

03 / NAVIGATE & DISCOVER COMMUNITIES EST. 2 MINS

Navigate & Discover Communities

Viva Engage Communities are spaces where Associates can connect and participate in conversations around shared topics as a group.

04 / INTERACT WITH POSTS EST. 2 MINS

Interact with Posts

Interact with posts by responding to prompts, commenting, reacting, and more.

05 / MANAGE YOUR NOTIFICATIONS EST. 2 MINS

Manage Your Notifications

Customize your Viva Engage notifications to control how and when you receive updates.
